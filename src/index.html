When does Ramadan start?

Ramadan is the ninth month of the Islamic calendar. The dates shown here are calculated in advance. In practice, the start of the month is confirmed by a moon sighting or by your country's official calendar, so the actual first day of fasting where you live can differ by a day. Follow your local mosque or religious authority for the final announcement.

Suhoor and iftar times

The fast runs from dawn to sunset: suhoor ends at the start of Fajr, and iftar is at Maghrib. The full Ramadan {{RAMADAN_YEAR}} timetable on QiblaFind lists these for every day of the month, and the prayer times page gives today's times for your location.

Frequently asked questions

When is Ramadan {{RAMADAN_YEAR}} expected to start?

The widget shows the calculated start date. The exact first day depends on the moon sighting or official announcement in your country.

Why do countries sometimes start Ramadan on different days?

Some countries rely on a local moon sighting and others on a calculated or official calendar, so the first day can differ by a day between countries.

How long is Ramadan?

Ramadan lasts 29 or 30 days, depending on when the new moon for the following month is sighted or calculated.
